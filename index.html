League Rules — 7’s Heaven

  • How to win: If any of your four teams score 17, 27, or 37 points in a week, you win.
  • Teams: Each player has 4 teams — 2 AFC and 2 NFC.
  • Multiple winners: Pot splits evenly among all winners that week.
  • No winner: Pot rolls over to next week.
  • Weekly entry: $20 per player.
  • Progressive pot: If nobody wins, the pot increases by $20 the following week and continues to grow until someone hits 7’s Heaven.
  • Week 18: If there’s still no winner by Week 18, the pot is returned evenly to all players.

This league uses 7’s Heaven mode. Edit this text in index.html if your house rules differ.
